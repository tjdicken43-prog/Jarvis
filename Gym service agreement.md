**SecurityAI service agreement**

Between [TJ's business name, once the LLC exists] ("we") and [Gym name, address] ("the gym"). Starts [date].

1. **What we do.** During the hours the gym picks (default 9 PM to 5 AM) a small box by the gym's recorder counts people at [entrance] and compares each entry with front-desk check-ins. When more people walk in than scanned, the gym gets a photo and the time. A person on the gym's staff decides what each flag means.
2. **What we don't do.** No facial recognition. No identifying anyone. No video stored. We don't touch the gym's check-in system or computers; we read a copy of the front-desk screen.
3. **Equipment.** The gym pays [$X] for the kit at our cost, and it belongs to the gym. We set it up and keep its software updated. The gym gives it power, network and a spot by the recorder.
4. **Pilot.** The first [30] days are free. Either side can stop during the pilot with no charge for service.
5. **Price.** After the pilot, $200 a month per entrance, +$100 a month for each extra entrance or gym, billed monthly in advance. Month to month; either side can cancel with [30] days' written notice (email counts).
6. **Data.** Photos are deleted after 48 hours. Flagged entries, and any the gym marks as a tailgate, are kept 35 days for the gym's monthly report, then deleted. The gym can pick shorter times in its settings. Member numbers are scrambled on the box and never sent as text. The data is the gym's; we use it only to run the service and never sell it. If we ever learn of a data breach we tell the gym within [72 hours].
7. **Gym's part.** Post the [[Camera privacy notice]] at the door, in the member area, and in the gym's membership terms. Make sure its own camera use is lawful. Keep the box plugged in.
8. **Not a guard.** The service helps staff spot entries that didn't scan. It can miss people and it can flag entries that turn out fine. It is not a security guard, alarm or life-safety system, and the gym stays responsible for safety at its site.
9. **Limit.** Our total responsibility under this agreement is capped at the fees the gym paid in the [3] months before the claim. Neither side is liable for lost profits.
10. **Ending.** When the service ends we turn off the gym's account and delete its stored data within [30] days. The kit stays with the gym.
11. **Law.** Arkansas law. [County] County, Arkansas courts.

Signed: ______ for the gym, date ______ · ______ for us, date ______
