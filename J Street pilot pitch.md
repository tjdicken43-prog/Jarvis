# J Street pilot pitch

Draft for the owners of [[J Street Gym]]. Numbers in brackets get filled from the pilot. Prices from [[Pricing]]; any change goes in [[Decisions]] first.

## The pitch (one page)
**The problem.** People follow a member in without scanning. Every one is a lost membership, and staff can't watch the door all night.

**What SecurityAI does.** A small box by the camera recorder counts people at the door during the night hours and checks each entry against the front-desk check-ins. No video stored, no facial recognition, member numbers scrambled. See [[How SecurityAI works]].

**The pilot.** First month free. Every flagged entry is checked by a person, so the count is confirmed tailgates, not guesses.

**What we found.** [X] confirmed sneak-ins in [Y] nights, about [Z] a month.

**The safety side.** Someone who follows a member in signed no waiver. If they get hurt, the insurer asks how they got in. SecurityAI gives the gym a record that it checks the door.

**The math.** A sneak-in is worth about $10 here. SecurityAI is $200 a month per entrance, month to month. Catching 20 a month covers it; at [Z] a month that's about $[Z × 10] of lost membership found.

**The ask.** Keep it running at $200 a month, cancel any time.
