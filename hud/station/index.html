FAQ

// straight answers
What does it cost to run?

About $12 a year for the domain plus about $7 a month for the inbox (check). The bots run free on GitHub and the agents run on TJ's Claude plan. Stripe charges only when a client pays.

Can it email someone without TJ knowing?

No. First emails, replies, proposals, prices and refunds all wait for TJ's tap. Follow-ups are approved with the first email and stop the moment someone replies, bounces or opts out.

Why are the emails so short?

Data. Hunter looked at about 34 million cold emails: 20–39 words got the most replies (4.5%), with 60–99 close behind. Gong's study of 304,174 emails found a simple "interested?" question beats asking for a meeting.

Is it legal to cold email businesses?

Yes, under CAN-SPAM, if every email has a real postal address, an honest subject, and a working opt-out honored within 10 business days. The station enforces all three. No cold texts and no robocalls, ever.

Are the workers really walking around?

The walking is a show, like watching your crew in a game. What's real: the job cards, the counts and the logs. A real job shows up on your gold desk only when it truly exists.

Where's the off switch?

"Pause everything" sits at the top of the station app. The Overseer also pauses sending by itself if bounces pass 2% or anyone marks spam.
