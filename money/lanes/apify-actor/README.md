# Local Trade Business Finder

Lists plumbers, HVAC companies and roofers in an area, from OpenStreetMap. Turn on **Only businesses with no website listed** to see who has no site or only a social page.

**What you get per business:** name, trade, phone, website, address, coordinates and the OpenStreetMap id.

**Where the data comes from:** OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). Every row carries the attribution. OpenStreetMap is incomplete: a business missing from the results may still exist, and "no website" means none was listed there, not that none exists.

**What it does not do:** it does not scrape websites, social profiles or people. It does not collect email addresses. Using the list to contact businesses is your responsibility: follow CAN-SPAM for email and never send marketing texts or robocalls without consent.

**Area size:** keep the box under 4 square degrees, about a metro area. Larger boxes are refused so the free OpenStreetMap servers are not hammered.

**Pricing:** pay per result.
