The job

Lines

Check your state's rules on which parts of a job are taxable. In Arkansas that depends on whether the work is a taxable service. Ask your accountant or the state revenue office if unsure.

How it works

Each line is a quantity times a unit price. Lines marked Material get your markup added. Labor and other lines do not. Tax is figured on the lines you mark taxable, after markup. The deposit is a percentage of the total, and the balance is what is left.

The sheet is plain on purpose: your business name, the job, the lines, the total and what is due up front. Save as PDF from the print dialog and email it. A saved estimate stays in this browser only. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so keep a PDF of anything you sent.
