Front Desk

A phone assistant for plumbing, HVAC and roofing shops. It answers when you cannot.

What it does

When a customer calls after hours, or while you are on a job, the assistant picks up. It asks for the name, the number, the address and what is wrong, and marks it urgent if it sounds like a burst pipe, no heat in winter, or a roof open to the rain. You get a text and an email with the details, and a short summary each morning.

It tells callers it is an automated assistant if they ask. It does not quote prices or promise a time you did not give it. If you have set visit windows, it can offer them.
