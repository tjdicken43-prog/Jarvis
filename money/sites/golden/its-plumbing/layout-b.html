Water where it shouldn't be? Do this first

  1. Shut off the water

    The main shut-off is usually where the water line comes into the house: a garage, crawlspace, utility room or next to the water heater. Turn the handle clockwise, or turn the lever across the pipe.

  2. If it's the water heater

    Close the cold-water valve on top of the tank. Turn off its breaker, or set a gas heater to Pilot.

  3. Keep water away from power

    Move cords and keep clear of outlets near the water.

  4. Call us

    (479) 866-6310, Monday to Friday, 8 to 5. After hours, send a request with photos.

Smell gas?Leave the house now. Don't flip switches. Call 911 or your gas company from outside.

What's going on?

Pick the problem for what to try and when to call.

  • Try a plunger first.
  • Skip chemical drain cleaners. They are hard on pipes and on whoever opens the line next.
  • More than one drain slow at once points to the main line. Call us.
  • Electric heater: check its breaker.
  • Gas heater: check the pilot, following the steps on the tank's label.
  • Water around the base, or a tank older than 10 years? Time to talk about a new one.
  • Close the valve under the sink or behind the toilet.
  • If you can't find one, shut off the main.
  • Put a bucket down and take a photo for us.
  • Jiggle the handle. A worn flapper is the usual cause.
  • Still running? Close the valve behind the toilet until it's fixed. A running toilet can waste a lot of water.
  • One faucet only: unscrew and rinse the aerator at the tip.
  • The whole house: call us. It can be a valve, the pressure regulator or a hidden leak.

Is your water heater on its way out?

Signs it's time to replace it, before it fails on a cold morning:

  • It's over 10 years old

    The date is usually on the label on the side of the tank.

  • Rusty or brown hot water

    Rust in the hot water can mean the tank is rusting inside.

  • Rumbling or popping

    Sediment has built up in the bottom of the tank.

  • Water around the base

    A leaking tank can't be patched. Plan a replacement.
