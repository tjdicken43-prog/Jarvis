After a hail or wind storm

  1. Stay off the roof

    Look from the ground. Take photos of missing shingles, dents in gutters and vents, and any water inside.

  2. Stop the water inside

    Move what you can, put a bucket down. If water is coming through a light fixture, turn off that breaker.

  3. Call us for a look

    We have 24/7 emergency response for storm damage: 479-426-7931.

  4. Call your insurance company soon

    Many policies set a time limit for storm claims. Your photos and our notes on the damage help.
