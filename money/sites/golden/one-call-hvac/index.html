Before you call: quick checks

Pick what your system is doing. Some fixes take two minutes.

  • Thermostat on Heat, set a few degrees above the room.
  • Put fresh batteries in the thermostat.
  • Check the furnace breaker, and the switch near the furnace that looks like a light switch.
  • Check the filter. A clogged filter can shut a furnace down.
  • Thermostat on Cool and fan on Auto.
  • Check both breakers: one for the indoor unit, one for the outdoor unit.
  • Change a dirty filter.
  • Ice on the copper lines? Turn cooling off, set the fan to On to thaw it, and call us.
  • Change the filter first. It is the most common cause.
  • Open closed vents and clear anything blocking the return grilles.
  • Ice on the lines or a fan that won't spin? Turn the system off and call.
  • Turn the system off at the thermostat.
  • Wipe up the water and keep it away from cords and outlets.
  • A blocked drain line is a common cause. Call and we'll clear it.
  • Grinding, screeching or a burning smell: turn the system off at the thermostat and call.
  • Don't keep running it. Small problems get expensive fast.

Smell gas (like rotten eggs)?Leave the house now. Don't flip switches. Call 911 or your gas company from outside.

Have this ready when you call

  1. What it is doing

    No heat, no cooling, a noise, water. And when it started.

  2. The brand and age

    It's on the sticker on the indoor or outdoor unit. A phone photo works.

  3. Any blinking lights or codes

    Many furnaces show a code through a small window on the panel.

  4. Your filter size

    Printed on the edge of the filter, like 16x25x1.

Geothermal, explained

A geothermal heat pump uses the steady temperature underground to heat your home in winter and cool it in summer. We install ClimateMaster geothermal heat pump systems. Ask us if your property is a fit.
