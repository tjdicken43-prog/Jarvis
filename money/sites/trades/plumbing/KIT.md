# Plumbing kit

Reference build: `../../golden/its-plumbing/index.html`.

## Who visits and why
- **Water on the floor:** panicking. Needs "shut off the water" steps on the first screens and a phone number.
- **No hot water or a clog:** wants to know what to try and when it's worth a call.
- **Old water heater:** wondering if it's time. That's a big-ticket job for the owner.
- **Businesses:** need to know commercial work is offered.

## What the owner wants
Less damage before they arrive, photos with requests, honest after-hours expectations, and water heater replacements booked before they fail.

## Page order (emergency trade: call first)
1. Hero: "Plumbing repair and installs in <town>." License number and true hours, a big call button, and a request form card with **photos**.
2. **Water where it shouldn't be? Do this first:** 4 steps plus the gas-smell alert.
3. **What's going on?** Problem picker: Clogged drain, No hot water, Leak, Running toilet, Low pressure.
4. Reviews, then The work.
5. **Is your water heater on its way out?** 4 signs plus a CTA, and FAQ.
6. Contact with a map link. Sticky Call / Request bar.

## Reusable tool copy (general, safe)
- Main shut-off: usually where the line enters (garage, crawlspace, utility room, by the water heater). Turn the handle clockwise, or the lever across the pipe.
- Water heater: close the cold valve on top, then turn off its breaker or set gas to Pilot.
- Clog: plunger first; skip chemical cleaners; several slow drains point to the main line.
- No hot water: breaker (electric) or pilot per the tank label (gas). A leaking base or a tank over 10 years old means talk replacement.
- Leak: fixture valve, else the main; bucket and a photo. Running toilet: flapper; close the valve behind it. Low pressure: one faucet means the aerator; the whole house means call.
- Water heater signs: over 10 years old (label), rusty hot water, rumbling or popping, water at the base.

## Facts to collect
License number (Arkansas master plumber numbers look like MP-####), phone, address, true hours and after-hours policy, towns, services (drains, water heaters, tankless, gas lines, sewer, repipes, commercial), reviews with names.

## Never claim unless sourced
24/7 or emergency service, prices, "free" estimates, tankless brands, camera inspections, hydro-jetting.
